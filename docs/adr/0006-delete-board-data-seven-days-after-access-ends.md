# Delete board data seven days after access ends

Board Access expires after twelve months during the Pilot, and a Teacher may end it earlier. Expiry and early termination remove access immediately and permanently delete the Managed Board seven days later. Deactivating a Teacher removes their access immediately and places their Personal Board and all Managed Boards on the same deletion schedule.

## Consequences

The Pilot does not provide a recovery button during the seven-day delay, but the delay keeps that option open and prevents storage from growing indefinitely. Regenerating a Teacher Access Link is a separate operation: it replaces the credential while preserving the Teacher dashboard and every board.
