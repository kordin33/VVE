# Keep a disconnected Managed Board read-only in the MVP

Students may edit a Managed Board online without the Owning Teacher being present. When the client loses its connection or has not completed initial synchronization, the board becomes read-only while navigation and export remain available. Full offline editing and later synchronization are deferred until after the MVP.

## Consequences

Revocation immediately removes server access. The MVP does not promise continued access to Student Work that was not exported before expiry or revocation, although a future offline copy may preserve that material without consuming server resources.
