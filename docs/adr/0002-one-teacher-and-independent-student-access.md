# Give each Managed Board one Teacher and independent Student access

Every Managed Board has exactly one Owning Teacher and one or more Students. Students may access and edit the board while the Owning Teacher is absent, but no Teacher can grant another Teacher access or transfer ownership. This is a business rule of the tutoring company, not an initial product limitation.

## Consequences

A Board Access Link may be shared by a student group, but it never grants Teacher authority. Infrastructure must reduce the cost of idle boards without making Student access depend on Teacher presence.
